About Our Team

Matrix 9013 is a team that usually houses 7th through 9th graders from Edgewood but we are open to anyone wanting to join. Our team resides in Merritt Island at Merritt Island Highschool, portable 1. Our team includes multiple different sectors or fields of work such as mentoring, electrical, mechanical, and programming. First is an international robotics organization with three different challenges. FTC offers the First Lego League, First Tech Challenge, and First Robotics Competition.

Team Past

We had started as 2 Lego Leagues in 2014 called Scrap Metal and Space Chocolate. Scrap Metal and our sister team, Horsepower had eventually merged in order to educate more experienced players and been promoted into a team of the First Tech Challenge. Our name, 9013 Matrix, had come from one of the original mentors of the Lego League, Mrs. Lesie Leutbecker, who currently runs the club.

The team dealing with mechanics are in charge of concepting the design of the robot and building the structure of the robot from the ground up. The team is currently working on prototypes in Onshape. Mechanics also wire and secure connections throughout the frame of the robot, allowing it to use its motors and other mechanisms.

Game

This year's game has two alliances of two robots competing to score the most points. The field has two 'hives' in which the robots will attempt to score 'pollen' and 'nectar'. Three 'nectar' start in each upward-facing hive cell, and when enough pollen is launched into the hive, it will 'tip', earning points and allowing access to the pollen and nectar in the hives. Every hive tip allows the HP to add one additional nectar to the field. At the end of the match, the 'flowers' are also scored. Teams earn points if they have one of their nectar at the bottom of the flower, and/or at the top.

Game times and Points

There are 3 time zones in the match: Autonomous, Teleop, and End game. Autonomous is the first 30 seconds of the match, and during it, robots are not controlled by the drivers, but instead they follow a pre-programmed routine. During Auto, robots can earn points by tipping the hives (20 pts), leaving the starting zone (3 pts), and parking in the loading zone (5 pts). Additionally, the alliance will earn the 'Swarm Ranking Point' if the allience has ≥ 16 points from leaving and parking. After Autonomous is completed, there is an 8-second transition window to TeleOp. During TeleOp, the robots become controlled by the drivers, who should continue launching pollen and nectar into the hives. The flowers will not be scored until the End game period, and is is actually a major foul (15 pts to opposing alliance) to score nectar into the flowers before then. Once the game ends, all robots are to stop moving and flowers are scored. Flowers are scored by nectar is at the bottom of the flower (5pts) and if the topmost element in the flower is a nectar (3 pts per element in the flower). Further game info can be found at the game manual.
